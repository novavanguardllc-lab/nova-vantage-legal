Delete Your Account
⚠️
This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data from Nova Vantage servers. Your YouTube channel is not affected — only your Nova Vantage account and associated data will be deleted.

What gets deleted
  • Your Nova Vantage account and authentication record
  • Channel profile data (name, handle, avatar) stored on our servers
  • Saved swipe file items and content you've saved in the app
  • Notification preferences and quiet hours settings
  • Push notification token
  • All usage data and session history associated with your account
What is not affected

Your Google account and YouTube channel are completely unaffected. Nova Vantage only reads your YouTube data — it does not store your videos, comments, or any content from your channel beyond basic profile information.

Deleting your Nova Vantage account does not revoke Google OAuth access. To fully disconnect, also visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Nova Vantage from your connected apps.
