Nova Vantage doesn't make content. It makes creators.

What It Does

Nova Vantage is an AI-powered companion app for YouTube creators, built by Nova Vanguard LLC. It helps creators research verified sources, draft scripts in their own voice, generate SEO packages, fact-check claims, and track performance — all without generating fake voiceovers, AI video, or avatars. The content is always the creator's own.

Core Features

  • Verified-source research assistant
  • Script generation in the creator's own voice
  • SEO title & description optimization
  • Built-in fact checking
  • YouTube channel performance tracking

Your Data

Nova Vantage connects securely to your YouTube account via Google's official OAuth sign-in. We only request the permissions needed to power the features above, and you can revoke access at any time from your Google Account settings.
